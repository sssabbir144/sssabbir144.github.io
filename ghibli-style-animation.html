Frequently Asked Questions

Is this official Studio Ghibli animation?

No — this is original, anime-inspired work with a hand-crafted, serene look. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Studio Ghibli; the style simply shares that love of quiet, everyday beauty.

What is this style best for?

Calm brand stories, food and nature content, wellness topics and gentle children's stories — anywhere a peaceful, trustworthy feeling matters more than noise.

Can you make a longer film in this style?

Yes, runtimes are flexible. Share your idea and the scope, and you will get a clear plan and schedule before any work begins.
