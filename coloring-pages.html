Free Printable Coloring Pages for Kids

Color the characters your kids already love from the films. Every sheet here is drawn from one of my animated short films — a friendly mosquito, a cake-loving bunny, a brave little bird — redrawn as clean, bold line art that small hands can actually finish.

The first printable sheets are being prepared now and will appear on this page character by character. Each sheet is free to print at home, and free for teachers to print for their classroom.

How It Works

1. Pick a character. Choose a favorite from the collection below — each card shows the age guide and how detailed the sheet is.

2. Print or download. Every finished sheet opens as a print-ready page: tap Print to print it straight from your browser, or Download PDF to keep it for later and print as many copies as your class needs.

3. Color and enjoy. Thick outlines, big open shapes, and no tiny fiddly corners — made for crayons, markers and colored pencils, and for the proud fridge-door finish.

Questions Parents & Teachers Ask

When can we print the first coloring sheets?

The printable line-art sheets are being prepared now, character by character, and the first set will appear on this page as soon as each sheet is ready. Until a sheet is live, its card is marked “coming soon” — the film character is already here so you can pick your favorites.

Are the coloring pages free for home and classroom use?

Yes. Every sheet published on this page will be free to print at home and free for teachers to print for their own classroom. The sheets are for personal and classroom use — please do not sell them or re-upload them as your own.

What do we need to print a coloring page?

Any home or school printer works. Each sheet is made for A4 and US Letter paper, with thick, clear outlines so crayons, markers and colored pencils all stay inside the lines. Younger children do best with crayons or thick markers.

Which ages are these coloring pages for?

Most sheets are designed for ages 3 to 8: very easy pages with big shapes for preschoolers, and a few medium pages with more detail for early elementary children. Each card shows its age guide and difficulty.
