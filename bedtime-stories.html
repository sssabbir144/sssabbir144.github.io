Questions Parents & Teachers Ask

How long is each bedtime story?

Each story is written to be read aloud in about five minutes — roughly 400 words, in short, calm paragraphs with gentle language that settles rather than excites. Long enough to feel like a real story, short enough for sleepy eyes.

What ages are these bedtime stories for?

The stories are written for children aged about 3 to 7. Younger children enjoy them read aloud at bedtime; early readers can start reading them aloud themselves.

Can teachers read these stories in class?

Yes — the stories are welcome as read-aloud material in classrooms and story-time groups. Each story page also has a short lesson section and a few questions, which work well for a calm end-of-day discussion.
