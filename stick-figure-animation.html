Stick Figure Animation

Black-and-white and colorful stick figure shorts with classic slapstick timing, snappy gags and expressive minimal acting — proof that a simple line can carry a whole story when the timing is right.

From rooftop kite duels to a haunted night train, these stick figure films are built for fast laughs, sudden scares and endings viewers want to share. Based in Bangladesh, working with clients and audiences worldwide.

What You Get

Slapstick timing, minimal lines

Classic cartoon timing in its purest form — every gag lands because the movement, not the detail, does the work.

Comedy, horror & story shorts

Stick figures carry more than jokes: rivalries, friendships and even a one-eyed ghost on a night train.

Made for Shorts & Reels

Series-friendly characters

How It Works

1. Story & Script

Your idea becomes a short, clear story with a beginning, a turn and an ending your audience will remember.

2. Characters & Style

Original characters are designed in the chosen style — and stay consistent in every scene that follows.

3. Animation & Sound

Every scene is animated with expressive acting, then finished with music and sound effects that carry the mood.

4. Review & Delivery

You review the finished film, and it is delivered in the format your platform needs — ready to publish.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why choose stick figure animation?

It is fast, funny and memorable: pure timing and acting with no distractions. Stick figure shorts are perfect for comedy channels, Shorts and Reels, and series with returning characters.

Can stick figures carry a scary or serious story?

Absolutely — the horror short The Ghost on the Night Train shows how a simple line figure, the right lighting and sound can hold real tension and a cliffhanger ending.

What formats do you deliver?

Vertical cuts for YouTube Shorts, Reels and TikTok, and landscape versions for regular YouTube videos — planned from the start so nothing feels cropped or rushed.
